# 📘 English Grammar

**Grammar practice for Ukrainian and Russian speakers who already speak English but want to stop making the same mistakes.** Short rules, lots of typed and spoken practice, feedback on every mistake, and spaced review of the patterns that go wrong.

Website: **https://appleinfire.github.io/EnglishGrammar/** (made for iPhone and iPad; install it with Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*, and it works offline).

Built on the family app shell from [Math Expedition](https://github.com/appleinfire/Math) (`templates/app-shell/`), like [WritingPower](https://github.com/appleinfire/WritingPower).

## How it works

- **37 topics** (≈1 800 exercises) from A2 to C1, grouped by level and chosen for the typical mistakes of Ukrainian and Russian speakers:
  - **A2:** present simple and continuous, past simple and continuous, *some / any / much / many*, comparatives, prepositions, *can / must / have to*, questions and *do*.
  - **B1:** Present Perfect, Past Simple vs Present Perfect, *for / since / ago*, Present Perfect Continuous, *used to*, Past Perfect, the future, polite and indirect questions, articles, relative clauses, *should / might*, phrasal verbs, linking words, *too / enough*, *if*, the passive, reported speech, *-ing* or *to*.
  - **B2:** third and mixed conditionals, *wish*, *must have / can't have*, future continuous and perfect, advanced passive (*have it done, it is said*), reporting verbs, advanced articles.
  - **C1:** inversion, cleft sentences (*What I need is…*), participle clauses, the subjunctive (*I suggest he go, it's time we left, I'd rather you didn't*), *should have / needn't have / was supposed to*.
- **🎬 Situations:** 12 short dialogs from real life, played as a chat — *Life in the US* (booking a doctor's appointment, returning a jacket, calling the insurance company, a leaking dishwasher and the landlord, the pharmacy), *School & kids* (a message to the teacher, a parent-teacher conference, summer camp), *Work* (a job interview, catching up with a colleague), *People & fun* (small talk with a neighbor, back from Yellowstone). Her lines are exercises of the usual types, each training a pattern of a grammar topic: a mistake gets that topic's explanation, counts for the topic, and comes back in Review as an ordinary sentence of the same pattern. At the end: the whole dialog, *How people say it* (useful phrases with 🔊 and 🌐) and *Good to know* (a practical note: copay, pre-approval, store credit, small talk…). **🎭 Say your lines** replays the dialog: their lines are read aloud, she says hers, and 🎙 checks them.
- **💡 Did you know:** 131 sentences in the exercises are true facts (*The Statue of Liberty was given to the US by France. A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus. Octopuses have three hearts.*), in four themes: *The US & the world*, *Science & space*, *Nature & animals*, *People & everyday life*. Each one trains a pattern of its grammar topic, at least 2 per topic, chosen so the grammar fits the fact (passive for inventions, the third conditional for discoveries, superlatives for records, *used to* for old beliefs, myths and how they are wrong). Facts are ordinary sentences of their topic, so they come up in lessons, review, Challenge and Endless. After the answer: **💡 Did you know?** with a line or two more (🌐 translation). A right answer puts the fact in her **collection** (*27 of 131 found*), sorted by theme, with 🔊; **Discover more** starts Endless with facts only, the ones not found first.
- **♾️ Endless:** exercises never run out. Topics are mixed (my topics = the started ones, else the topics of her level; or chosen), weaker topics and topics with more mistakes come more often, never the same topic twice in a row, and every 4th exercise is a pattern due for review (🔁). Every topic keeps its own tier: 3 right in a row → up, 2 mistakes → down; the next lesson of the topic starts there (passing a tier still needs a lesson). Sentences: *Grammar*, *Situations* (dialog lines, with what was said just before), *Mixed* or *💡 Facts* (from all topics, those not found first). A run counter 🔥 and records: the longest run of right answers, and the most right in a **⚡ Lightning** round of 2 minutes. *Stop* shows the results.
- **English first, translation on demand.** Rules and explanations are in simple English; the 🌐 button shows them in Ukrainian or Russian (Settings). Exercise sentences open in Google Translate.
- **Rules: a cheat sheet, and an explanation behind every line.** A topic's rule is a short list, one line per point; *Explain ▾* under a line opens a longer, plain explanation of it: the idea, where the mistake comes from (Ukrainian / Russian), right and wrong examples side by side (✅ ❌), and how to check yourself — with its own 🌐.
- **Three tiers in every topic:** **Core** (the form, with hints) → **Use** (in context, no hints) → **Pro** (translate from Ukrainian/Russian, fix a mistake, rewrite, say it). A topic is mastered only at Pro. Inside a lesson the tier moves up after 3 right answers in a row and down after 2 mistakes.
- **Exercise types:** choose (only as a warm-up), fill in the blank, put the words in order, find and fix the mistake, rewrite, translate, listen and type, say it out loud.
- **Feedback after every answer:** her answer next to the right one with the differences marked, an explanation of *that* mistake (🌐 translation), 🔊 listen / 🐢 slowly, 🎙 record yourself and compare, "Why? Rule" (in a topic with explanations it opens the explanation of the point this exercise trains, or of the mistake she made, with *All rules of the topic* below). Translations can be said many ways, so *My answer was right too* counts an answer the app did not know.
- **Checking answers** runs on the device: contractions equal full forms (*I've = I have*), iPhone quotes and capitals don't matter, a one-letter typo in a long word is forgiven, grammar endings (*-s, -ed*, *began / begun*) are not. Autocorrect is off in answer fields so the phone doesn't fix the grammar.
- **American English is the norm:** American voice, and answers like *Did you eat yet? / I just finished* are accepted with a note about the textbook form.
- **Spaced review:** every pattern she practices becomes a card (Leitner boxes: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days). A mistake brings the card back today; a card comes back with a **different sentence of the same pattern**. **Review** mixes all topics.
- **Level check** (adaptive: one Pro exercise per topic, level by level, stops when a level is too hard) sets your level and a starting tier for every topic. **Challenge** mixes the started topics with no hints and an optional 40-second timer. **Topic check** (Pro, 75% to pass).
- **My mistakes:** the types of mistakes made most often, each with an explanation and a *Practice this* button; recent mistakes with the right sentence.
- **Progress:** answers, accuracy, active days, streak (one missed day a week is forgiven), mistakes fixed, patterns learned, minutes per day for 30 days, tiers per topic. A daily goal (5–30 minutes) on the home screen.
- **Weekly report** (Monday to Sunday): minutes, answers, accuracy, active days, mistakes fixed, patterns learned, tiers passed — each compared with the week before; minutes by day against the daily goal; the topics practiced with their accuracy; the most frequent mistakes with *Practice this*; one focus for the next week (the weakest topic, else the most frequent mistake). Last week's report waits on the home screen until it is opened; older weeks are one tap back. *Share the report* sends it as text (iPhone share sheet, or copied).

## Voice

| What | How | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Listen | `speechSynthesis` — Siri voices on iPhone/iPad (choose one in Settings; download *Enhanced / Premium* voices in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content) | free |
| Record yourself and compare | `MediaRecorder`, nothing leaves the device | free |
| Say it out loud | 🎙 next to every sentence answer (translate, rewrite, fix, *Say it*): the browser's speech to text (`SpeechRecognition`; Safari on iOS 14.5+ with Siri or Dictation on, Chrome). She sees what was heard and can edit it before *Check*. Every version the recognizer offers is tried; articles and endings it may mishear do not count as mistakes, but reading back the wrong sentence of a *fix* exercise never passes. If speech to text is not allowed (some home-screen apps on iOS), the button hides and the keyboard microphone (iOS dictation) does the same job. Can be turned off in Settings. | free (the sound goes to Apple or Google for recognition) |
| Later | pronunciation scores with Azure Pronunciation Assessment through a small proxy | Azure: about 5 free audio hours a month |

`aiProxy` in `js/config.js` is reserved for a later "Explain my mistake" button (Claude through a Cloudflare Worker); with `null` nothing is shown.

## Same progress on every device

Same as Math Expedition and WritingPower: **Create a family** (code + PIN) on the first device, **I already have a family** on the others, or **Use family …** with one tap if another of our apps is connected on the device. Each device keeps a full copy and works offline; copies are merged without losing anything:

| What | When two copies differ |
|---|---|
| Topics (best tier passed, lessons, answers, best topic check), mistake counts, minutes per day, the weekly-report log (per day, topic and mistake), seen sentences, dialogs (best score) | kept from both; the higher value wins |
| Facts found | kept from both; the first day wins |
| Review cards | kept from both; for one card, the copy answered more recently wins (a mistake can lower a box) |
| Totals (answers, right, fixed, sessions) and Endless records | the higher value |
| Current tier of a topic, settings, recent mistakes, the last Endless choices | the newer copy |

Mastery and topic status are not stored; they are computed from the cards and answers.

## Files

```
index.html             the page; plain <script> tags, no build step, no npm
css/style.css          all styles; light and dark tokens in :root; two columns from 900px (iPad landscape)
js/config.js           name, storage id 'english-grammar' (never change), levels, Firebase config, aiProxy
js/schema.js           what a profile holds and how two copies merge
js/content.js          the content registry and the format of a topic (see the comment at the top)
js/topics/*.js         one file per topic: rule (en/uk/ru) or points (a cheat-sheet line + an explanation each), typical mistake, error explanations (en/uk/ru), exercises
js/facts/*.js          one file per theme of Did-you-know facts: an exercise of a grammar topic plus a detail (en/uk/ru)
js/scenes/*.js         one file per dialog: intro, lines (her lines are exercises of a grammar topic), phrases, tip (en/uk/ru)
js/check.js            judging typed answers: contractions, quotes, typos, word-by-word comparison
js/srs.js              spaced repetition cards
js/level.js            tiers, lessons, review, level check, challenge, topic check, drills, Endless, dialogs; recording answers (and the day log)
js/report.js           the weekly report: one week's numbers, the week before, a focus, the text to share
js/i18n.js             🌐 translations, Google Translate links, the tiny text format
js/voice.js            read-aloud, record yourself, speech to text (🎙), sounds
js/exercises.js        how each exercise type looks and how an answer is read and judged
js/app.js              screens and routing (profiles, family, home, topics, session, results, mistakes, progress, settings)
js/util.js, js/store.js, js/cloud.js, js/vendor/   the app shell: helpers, profiles, Firebase sync, Firebase SDK 10.14.1
sw.js                  offline cache (every file is listed in FILES)
tests/                 see below
```

To add a topic: copy a file in `js/topics/`, add it to `index.html` and to `FILES` in `sw.js`, run `node tests/content.test.js`. The test checks three languages for every rule and explanation (and, for a topic with points, that every pattern and type of mistake is explained by exactly one point; and that every **bold** and *italic* mark pairs up), at least 10 exercises per tier and 3 per pattern, that every right answer is accepted and every listed wrong answer is not.

To add a fact: add it to a file in `js/facts/` (the format is at the top of `js/content.js`): an exercise with its topic (`g`), an existing pattern (`pt`), that topic's errors, a tier (`l`) and `f: { en, uk, ru }`. The content test checks it like any topic sentence, plus the three languages of the detail, at least 2 facts per topic and 25 per theme, and that no fact repeats a topic sentence.

To add a dialog: copy a file in `js/scenes/` (the format is described at the top of `js/content.js`), add it to `index.html` and to `FILES` in `sw.js`, run `node tests/content.test.js`. Every exercise names its grammar topic (`g`) and an existing pattern of it (`pt`); its errors are that topic's errors. The test checks this, three languages for the intro, phrases and tip, and the same answer checks as for topics.

## Tests

```sh
node tests/shell.test.js     # merging two copies, storage, save code, family key, offline file list
node tests/content.test.js   # every topic and exercise
node tests/report.test.js    # day log, weeks, comparison, focus, the home-screen reminder
node tests/check.test.js     # judging answers
node tests/srs.test.js       # review cards
node tests/level.test.js     # tiers and sessions, Endless, dialogs, facts
node tests/smoke.mjs         # in Chromium, iPhone and iPad sizes (needs Playwright)
node tests/cloud-e2e.mjs     # two "devices" syncing through the real Firebase project (needs Playwright and internet)
```

Every push to `main` runs the node tests and publishes the site to GitHub Pages (Actions → *Deploy to GitHub Pages*). One-time setup: Settings → Pages → Source: **GitHub Actions**.

To try it locally: `python3 -m http.server` and open http://localhost:8000 (family sync needs `http(s)://`).
